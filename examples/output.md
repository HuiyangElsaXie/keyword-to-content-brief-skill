# Page decisions — fictional demonstration

## Business question
With capacity for one new page, what should the team write next?

## Page map
- meeting notes template → update /templates/meeting-notes. Existing coverage should be checked and improved before a second general template page is created.
- meeting action items template → new /templates/action-items. A focused next-step worksheet is a plausible complementary resource. This is an editorial hypothesis, not confirmed search demand.
- meeting notes vs minutes → defer. Useful comparison idea, but the one-page capacity is already allocated.

## Brief: meeting action items template
Reader: a small-team lead leaving a meeting with ambiguous next steps.
Promise: a copyable structure for recording a task, owner, deadline and completion evidence.
Outline:
1. What belongs in an action item?
2. A blank four-field worksheet.
3. A clearly fictional worked example.
4. How to review unresolved ownership and deadlines.
5. When the general meeting-notes template is a better starting point.
Evidence needed: editorial review of the worksheet; no product efficacy claims.
Internal link: /templates/meeting-notes, only after verifying that page exists in the intended site.
Acceptance: copyable worksheet, explicit fictional sample, no fabricated search volume, no promise of higher rankings.

## Missing evidence and validation
All keywords are synthetic. No actual SERP, volume, ranking, or conversion data was collected. Before production, validate intent and existing coverage with authorized data. After publication, define a baseline and observe qualified visits and useful actions; no improvement is claimed here.
