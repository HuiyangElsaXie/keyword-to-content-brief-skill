# Adaptive business intake / 自适应业务提问

## EN: conversation, not a form wall
Read the brief first. Summarize supplied answers for correction; reuse them with provenance. Ask up to three missing essentials at a time. Combine related fields conversationally:
1. What industry/offer and audience is this for? Which geography and language?
2. What concrete problem or decision should the report help with, and what is the single primary objective? Awareness, consideration, conversion, retention or other? What tradeoff matters?
3. What stage is the brand/product at? Separately, how old is the site and what measurement history exists?
Then ask only needed follow-ups: brand promise and actual versus aspirational position; customer alternatives and supporting evidence; production/review capacity, budget/time, approval/legal/voice constraints. Self-reported positioning is acceptable when labeled; no market-share proof is demanded to receive a limited report.

Branch by the work:
- Awareness: reader's category/problem knowledge, intended association, credible proof, possible measurement. Do not substitute lead generation for awareness.
- Conversion: exact completed event (qualified enquiry, completed order, booked demo, etc.), audience, offer/landing destination, measurement and restrictions. A click is not necessarily conversion.
- Consideration: decision criteria, alternatives and permission/evidence for comparisons.
- Retention: customer stage, adoption/support job and verified support destination.
- Brand/nonbrand/competitor: whose entity, discovery versus reassurance/navigation, and whether comparative claims can be supported.
- Mixed intent/funnel unknown: clarify reader job; do not force an article.
- Existing content: supplied current body/review, what is missing, what is already adequate, evidence permission. Mapping alone is not a gap. Group work by URL without pretending distinct unverified queries form a cluster.
- Prelaunch/launch: avoid implied traction; mature/repositioning: check existing equity/coverage. Brand lifecycle and site age are separate.

An explicit “unknown” is retained and not repeatedly asked. The helper returns at most three unasked missing questions; the agent chooses the relevant branch. Unanswered essential context limits commissioning, not the ability to produce a useful report.

## Modes and report sequence
- tailored: essential business context is supplied and positioning at least explicitly qualified. Keyword/page evidence may still limit individual decisions.
- industry_general: industry supplied but concrete problem/objective or other essential context unavailable. Still useful reference: analyze supplied keywords/pages, outline alternative funnel uses, evidence gaps and validation questions. This is not a personalized ranked queue.
- generic_framework: no confirmed industry. Provide a general method and supplied-evidence organization, without industry-specific facts.

Every report: context and mode → evidence → recommendation/reference guidance → assumptions and limits → next validation. State which answers changed recommendations. Do not invent demand, traffic, benchmarks, positioning, competitors or business priorities. Agent/editor completes the report; generated scaffolds alone remain incomplete. A human reviews final work.

## 中文：先理解业务，再分析
先读取已有 brief，复用用户已提供的答案并注明来源；第一轮最多问三个缺失的关键问题，可合并行业/产品、受众/地区/语言、具体问题/首要目标。不要一次要求填完长问卷。
- 业务是什么、服务谁、在哪个市场、用什么语言？这份报告要帮助解决哪个具体问题？首要目标是知名度、考虑评估、转化、留存还是其他？哪些次要目标不能反客为主？
- 品牌/产品处于什么生命周期？网站年龄和测量历史单独记录，不能混为品牌成熟度。
- 品牌定位和竞争位置哪些有证据、哪些是自述或假设？客户还会考虑什么替代方案？不用先证明市场份额才能获得参考报告。
- 按需追问转化事件、现有页面覆盖、关键词品牌属性和漏斗角色、内容制作与审核资源、时间预算、合规及品牌表达限制。

目标决定提问：知名度关注认知与可信关联；评估关注选择标准和证据；转化必须定义具体完成事件，不能把跳转/点击当成交；留存关注已有客户任务。导航词先核对所指实体与页面，不能统一写成产品或帮助中心。混合意图先澄清任务。现有页面已覆盖就保留；只有映射关系时先审阅，不能直接要求改写。不同词映射同 URL 可以合并审阅任务，但不等于验证了关键词簇。

不知道可以明确回答 unknown。不会因为用户无法回答具体问题而拒绝给报告：
- 行业与必要业务上下文齐备：tailored 定制方向；关键词和页面证据不足仍会限制执行建议。
- 行业明确但无法提供具体问题、目标或其他必要上下文：industry_general 行业通用参考报告，仍有参考价值。整理已提供的关键词、页面承接、可能的目标用途、证据缺口与验证问题，不伪造公司专属优先级。
- 行业也不明确：generic_framework 通用分析框架，不能冒充行业研究。

报告顺序固定为：业务背景与报告层级 → 已提供证据 → 建议或参考方向 → 假设与限制 → 下一步验证。使用者自述、分析者假设和可核查证据分别标注，不虚构行业规律、竞品优势、搜索量或 ROI。

Brand/product lifecycle can differ: use brand_lifecycle and product_lifecycle separately when needed; lifecycle is the shared planning context, never inferred from website_stage. 品牌与产品阶段不同则分别填写 brand_lifecycle 和 product_lifecycle，不能用网站年龄推断。
